# Otter Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in the Otter community to be safe, respectful, and
productive for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or
role in the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and specific when giving feedback.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking the person behind them.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and the sensitivity of security work.
- Accept correction, acknowledge mistakes, and help repair harm.
- Keep examples, logs, screenshots, and test data free of real customer secrets.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, or publication of another person's
private or customer information is not acceptable. Using project spaces to
coordinate unauthorized security activity is also prohibited.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock discussions, reject contributions,
or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior conflicts
with this policy. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented,
and handled with discretion.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers using a private
channel exposed by the repository owner. Do not include sensitive details in a
public issue. Maintainers will limit access to reports and avoid retaliation
against good-faith reporters.

This policy applies to repository spaces and to public situations where someone
is officially representing the Otter project.
